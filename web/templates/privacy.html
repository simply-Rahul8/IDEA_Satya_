Privacy

Notice {{ notice_version }} · updated 13 November 2026 · Draft: not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

The short version

The claim checker reads the text you paste, checks it, and forgets it. We don't save it, log it, or send it to an AI model or anyone else. There are no ads, analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

The text you check

  • When you press Start analysis, your text goes to our server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • It is matched against Satya's written rules in memory, and the result comes straight back to your browser.
  • It is not written to a database or to our logs, and no AI model sees it. The check uses rules only.
  • Nothing is checked until you press the button. Pasting or typing stays in your browser.

What we do keep, and why

  • A session cookie (satya_sid): a random number that protects the checker form from cross-site attacks. It holds nothing about you and can't be read by scripts on the page.
  • A one-way scramble (hash) of your IP address, held in memory only for the rate-limit window (10 minutes), so one visitor can't overload the checker. We never store the IP address itself.
  • Your light or dark theme choice, saved in your own browser only if you switch it.

Hosting

Satya runs on Vercel, on servers in Mumbai, India. Like any web host, Vercel keeps standard request logs (IP address, page address, browser type and time) to run and secure the service; your pasted text is not part of them. See Vercel's privacy policy. Fonts and scripts are served from this site, not from third parties.

Brand accounts (not open yet)

Sign-in and the brand workspace are not open to the public. When they open, they will store your email address, your brand details and a record of what you agreed to. Sending product text to an AI model will be optional and off unless you tick it. This section will be completed, with how long data is kept, before accounts open.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or erase it. The checker keeps nothing that identifies you, so there is nothing to retrieve from it. For any privacy request or complaint, email rahuljinkachika46@gmail.com.
